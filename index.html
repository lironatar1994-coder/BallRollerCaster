How to play

🏗️

1 · Build

Pick Start Tower and click the floor, bed or desk. Then build with the arrows next to the glowing ring, or the pieces in the dock. ✨ finds the way into a goal.

▶️

2 · Play

Press Release (or Space). The 🔮 line shows where a ball will go — a red ring marks where it would fall off.

🎢

3 · Watch

Click a ball, then Ride inside it, or watch the Movie camera and Replay. Then try the 🏆 challenges!
